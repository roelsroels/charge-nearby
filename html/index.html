ABOUT THE DATA

Live enough to be useful.
Honest enough to say when it isn’t.

Postcodes are located through the public PDOK address register. Charger locations and connector availability are requested from EnBW mobility+ through this private server and briefly cached. Previously seen locations remain visible in gray when EnBW provides no current data. Availability is the roaming-network status—not a guarantee that a parking bay is accessible.
